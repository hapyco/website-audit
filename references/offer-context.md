# Hapy Astro migration offer

Verified against https://hapy.co/migrate-to-astro/ and the Hapy repository on 14 October 2026. Recheck the live offer before quoting price or scope.

The offer covers a page and URL inventory, reusable Astro templates, metadata/canonicals/sitemaps, forms and analytics, an agreed content editing setup, performance comparison, deployment to client accounts, code ownership, and a rollback plan. Migration starts at $1,499 in the inspected offer; the final quote depends on pages, templates, CMS and integrations. Hosting and paid services are separate. Do not treat this starting price as the client's quote.

The free audit is a useful public-site assessment. It is not the complete implementation inventory, penetration test, traffic forecast, or guarantee of rankings or AI citations.

## Fit

Astro can suit public marketing and content pages when smaller browser payloads, reusable templates, or simpler publishing address a demonstrated need. Confirm who edits content and which integrations must survive. Keep CRM, booking, payment and account systems where they work unless a separate change is justified.

Before a proposal, confirm traffic and converting URLs with the owner, map redirects, choose the CMS, inventory integrations, agree acceptance checks and rollback. Do not recommend deleting pages from public evidence alone.

## Three valid recommendations

- Repair the current site when form, content, link or configuration fixes address the main problems.
- Pilot Astro on one representative public page when performance or maintenance problems persist. Compare the same content, third-party tools and measurement settings.
- Scope migration when the pilot and business requirements support it. Separate migration from redesign and content work.

The inspected landing page asks for a migration plan and a 15-minute call. The sales campaign offers a free audit. Treat the audit as the first deliverable, with one next step to review findings. Do not change the landing page as part of this skill.
