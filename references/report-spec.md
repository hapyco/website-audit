Use 15 mm side margins and 13-14 mm vertical margins. Keep narrative text readable, normally 15-18 CSS px. Captions and methods may use 11-12 px. Use clear space between blocks and two columns only for short parallel findings. Do not split table rows. Do not hide overflow. Keep source records separate.

## Section 1 - Overview and visible evidence

Include business/domain, contact name, date, public-review scope, and one sentence with the main finding and its business implication. Show three priority findings, with the main screenshot directly after the leading finding. Avoid a repeated summary fact band. Use 1-2 real screenshots as separate HTML images with editable captions. A useful pair is the mobile first screen and the failed contact or booking section. Add concise captions with URL, viewport and finding. Include something that works and should be preserved.

Label the panel as lab results, with run count and collection date. Use one short sentence before the panel to explain the main finding and its effect; do not repeat every chart value in a paragraph. Include the page inventory by source and short blocks for SEO, sitemap structure, page inventory and security. Keep Design & UX and Agentic Browsing on page 2; keep observed AI visibility on page 4. Show status, evidence and business implication. Do not add client task columns. Missing data must remain visible rather than appearing as zero.
